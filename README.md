🎯 About the Project
Maintaining good health requires more than just counting calories or steps—it demands a holistic approach combining physical activity, nutrition, mindfulness, and sleep tracking. Pulse & Poise is built to bridge the gap between fragmented health metrics by providing a single, privacy-focused dashboard.

Core Objectives
Holistic Tracking: Unify physical fitness, hydration, sleep hygiene, and mood journaling into one platform.
Privacy First: All health data remains encrypted and stored locally by default.
Actionable Insights: Generate daily and weekly health reports with personalized improvement tips.

 Key Features
🏋️ Daily Activity Logging: Log workouts, cardio metrics, step count, and active calories.

🥗 Nutritional & Hydration Tracker: Track micro and macronutrient intake alongside a daily water intake log.

🧘 Mind & Mood Journaling: Record daily stress levels, mood patterns, and guided breathing sessions.

💤 Sleep Quality Analysis: Visualize sleep cycles (REM, deep, light) to improve daily recovery.

📊 Visual Analytics: Interactive charts showcasing health trends over time.
